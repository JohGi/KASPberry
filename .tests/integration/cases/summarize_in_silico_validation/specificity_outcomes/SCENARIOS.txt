MFEprimer specificity decision branches

Two KASP genotypes (A and B) are represented. A carries the first assay allele (A) and B the second allele (G).

SNPs / expected behaviour
- snp::1::100: exactly one target amplicon for the expected allele in both genotypes -> PASS.
- snp::1::200: genotype A has one expected amplicon, but the allele-specific primer ends one base away from the target SNP -> missing_target_amplicon.
- snp::1::300: genotype A has two expected amplicons, only one at the target -> multiple_expected_amplicons.
- snp::1::400: genotype A has the correct expected amplicon plus an amplicon from the other allele-specific primer -> unexpected_allele_amplicon.
- snp::1::500: genotype A has the correct target amplicon plus a noncanonical amplicon -> noncanonical_amplicon.

All correct hits use chromosome alias 2A__001 to verify that the source chromosome prefix 2A is recognized. No dimers or hairpins are present.
