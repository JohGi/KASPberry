Dimer and hairpin decisions

All assays have perfect specificity in both genotypes.

SNPs / expected behaviour
- snp::2::600: no intra-assay dimer or hairpin -> PASS. A cross-assay dimer involving one of its primers must be ignored.
- snp::2::700: an intra-assay dimer is reported -> FAIL with reason dimer.
- snp::2::800: a hairpin is reported -> FAIL with reason hairpin.

The dimer file also contains a dimer between primers belonging to assays 600 and 800. It must not invalidate either assay because cross-assay dimers are intentionally ignored.
