Basic compute_masked_block_n_stats integration case

Inputs
- One block (block 1) with two genotypes: SampleA and SampleB.
- Masked and unmasked alignments contain alignment gaps.
- SampleA has no pre-existing N and gains 2 repeat-masked Ns.
- SampleB has 2 pre-existing Ns and gains 2 additional repeat-masked Ns.
- Each biological sequence is 10 bp long after removing alignment gaps.

Expected behaviour
- Alignment gaps are excluded from sequence lengths and percentage denominators.
- Pre-existing Ns are not counted as repeat-masked bases.
- SampleA: 0.00% pre-existing N, 20.00% masked N, 20.00% repeat-masked bases.
- SampleB: 20.00% pre-existing N, 40.00% masked N, 20.00% repeat-masked bases.
